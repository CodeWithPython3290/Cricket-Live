🏏 CRICKET LEAGUE 3D

Time your shots, smash sixes and defend your total.

How to play Batting: press Space (or click) when the ball reaches the bat. Hold ←/→ to aim the shot, hold ↑ to loft it in the air (big runs but you can be caught), and hold ↓ to defend. The red ring shows where the ball will bounce.
Bowling: A/D move the line, W/S change the length, Q switches between fast and spin, and F bowls. Hit the stumps to bowl them!
2 Players: the batting side uses the arrow keys and Space, and the bowling side uses W A S D, Q and F.
